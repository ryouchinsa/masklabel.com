Support

Email support@masklabel.com. For bug reports, include your macOS version, your Mac model, the MaskLabel version, and the steps that led to the problem.

What do I need to run MaskLabel?

A Mac with Apple silicon. SAM3 runs through MLX, which requires it.

Which dataset formats are supported?

COCO and YOLO, for both import and export.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open MaskLabel ▸ MaskLabel Pro… and choose Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in to the Apple ID used for the purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open MaskLabel ▸ MaskLabel Pro… and choose Manage Subscription, or go to your Apple Account subscriptions. Pro stays unlocked until the end of the period you already paid for.

Can I use Pro on more than one Mac?

Yes. Purchases belong to your Apple Account. On another Mac, sign in with the same Apple Account and choose Restore Purchases in the MaskLabel Pro window.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and request a refund for the purchase.

Where can I find the shortcuts and help?

Read the Help page, which is the same as the Help window in the app. You can change any shortcut in Customize Shortcuts.

How do I clear cached data?

The Help window lists the cache files MaskLabel creates and how to delete them.
