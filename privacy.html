Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Your images and annotations stay on your Mac

MaskLabel runs SAM3 locally on your Mac with MLX. Your images, masks, class names and datasets are not uploaded to our servers, and we do not receive copies of them.

One-time model download

The first time you start MaskLabel, it downloads the SAM3 model from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Only the model is downloaded. No image, mask or dataset is sent. As with any download, Hugging Face can see your IP address. After this, the model loads from your Mac.

Importing an Ultralytics NDJSON dataset

If you import an Ultralytics NDJSON dataset file, MaskLabel downloads each image from the web address listed in that file and saves it on your Mac. These requests go to the servers that host those images, which can see your IP address. This happens only when you choose to import such a file. Nothing is uploaded: no image, mask or annotation is sent anywhere.

Data we collect

MaskLabel does not collect personal information, does not include advertising, and does not use third-party analytics or tracking.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to those transactions.

Files on your Mac

MaskLabel stores settings, shortcuts and cache files (such as thumbnails and folder listings) on your Mac. You can delete them at any time. The Help window lists where they are.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@masklabel.com.
